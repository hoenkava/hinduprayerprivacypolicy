This Privacy Policy explains how Hindu Prayer ("App", "we", "our", or "us"), operated by Puneet Raj, collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Hindu Prayer mobile application.

At a glance: Hindu Prayer uses Google Sign-In and Firebase services. Depending on the features you use, we process account details, Kundli birth information, Daily Journey entries, approximate location, app activity, and diagnostic information. We use Google AdMob for advertising. Cloudinary is not currently used.

2. Information We Collect

The information processed depends on the features you use and permissions you grant.

2.1 Google Sign-In and account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive and use your Google email address, name, profile photo (if provided), and Firebase user ID. We use these details to authenticate you, create and manage your account, associate your information with your account, and provide account features.

2.2 Kundli and birth details

If you use Kundli or related astrology features, you may provide your date of birth, exact time of birth, and place of birth. You confirmed these details are saved in Firebase. We use them to calculate or display Kundli and related astrology information. Birth information can be personal information; provide it only if you choose to use these features.

2.3 Daily Journey information

Daily Journey may process positive deeds or intentions, deadlines, obstacles, completion status, and personal reflections you enter. You confirmed this information is stored in Firebase and local device storage. We use it to provide the feature, retain entries, and display progress or history. Avoid entering sensitive information about yourself or others unless necessary and you are comfortable storing it.

2.4 Approximate location

You confirmed that the App accesses approximate location. Where enabled and permitted, we may use it for location-related features, such as geographically relevant calendar or Panchang information. This policy does not claim that the App collects precise GPS location. Access depends on device permissions and settings. You can manage location permission in your device settings; denying it may limit location-dependent features or require manual location entry.

2.5 App activity, diagnostics, and device information

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Depending on their configuration, these services may process app interaction and diagnostic information, such as configured app usage events, app version, device or operating-system information, crash reports, error details, and technical identifiers. The exact events and fields depend on the released App's Firebase configuration; this list does not mean every item is collected in every session.

2.6 Notifications

You confirmed that the App uses local device notifications only, not Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications. Local notifications may be scheduled on your device for reminders or App features. You can manage notification permission through device settings.

2.7 Support communications

If you email us, we receive the information you include, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond and address your request.
